Troubleshooting

Fix the buzz

Almost every beginner hits this in week one. It's rarely the guitar's fault.

  • 01

    Press just behind the fret, not on top of it

    Pressing too far back from the metal fret is the most common cause of a muted or buzzing note.

  • 02

    Curl your fingers, don't flatten them

    A flat finger can brush the string next to it. Arch from the knuckle so only the fingertip touches down.

  • 03

    Press with the fingertip, not the pad

    More pressure with less contact area gets a clean note with less finger fatigue.

  • 04

    Check your thumb

    A thumb hooked too high over the neck stops your fingers from arching properly — keep it roughly behind your middle finger.

Bonus

Your first barre chord: F

Every open-chord player hits this wall eventually. F is the hardest of the early chords because one finger has to press down two strings at once — but it unlocks a huge number of new songs once it clicks.

F
The red bar is your index finger laid flat across all six strings. The three dots are your other fingers, same shape as your open E chord — just moved up one fret.

Don't rush this one. Most players take 2–3 weeks of daily practice before F rings clean. Practice pressing and releasing it even without strumming — that alone builds the hand strength you need.
